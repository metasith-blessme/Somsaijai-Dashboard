Som Sai Jai Dashboard is an end-to-end sales intelligence and automated financial reporting platform for the Som Sai Jai Thai cold-press juice brand. It processes daily handwritten sales reports, POS receipts, and Thai banking transfer slips to maintain master Excel ledgers, audit daily reconciliations, allocate shared raw material costs, and generate live, interactive multi-branch management dashboards and P&L statements.
